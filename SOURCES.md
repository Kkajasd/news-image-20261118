## Front-page stories

1. **Iran strikes Kuwait and Bahrain** — Iran launched drones at the
   Ali Al Salem Air Base in Kuwait, which hosts US forces, and at
   targets in Bahrain, after US fighter jets struck an IRGC missile
   launch site on Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz. Both
   Gulf states say their air defences intercepted the attacks.
   (Just Security, Reuters, Wall Street Journal, One Turn News)
2. **Kerch Bridge span falls** — a second day of Ukrainian strikes on
   the bridge linking Russia to annexed Crimea; a road span collapsed
   into the strait. Russia hit two bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv in
   reply. (One Turn News, Daily Brief, BBC, Kyiv Independent)
3. **Trump lands in Shenzhen** — Xi Jinping greeted him at the airport,
   breaking protocol, ahead of Thursday's first-ever three-way talks
   with Xi and Vladimir Putin at APEC. Xi and Putin met in Shenzhen
   the same day. (Daily Brief, NPR, AFP, AP)
4. **Nvidia posts a record $115.8bn quarter** — revenue up 103% year on
   year, data centre $107.6bn; guides $130bn next quarter. The board
   said it does not intend to reinstate Jensen Huang as chief
   executive. (Nvidia, CNBC, Reuters, Decrypt)
5. **OpenAI launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol** — flagship API prices cut
   20% to $8/$40 per million tokens; Sol becomes ChatGPT's default
   engine. (OpenAI, SiliconANGLE, 9to5Mac)
6. **EU proposes a €45bn loan for Ukraine** — up to €25bn military and
   €20bn budget support for 2027; now goes to the European Parliament
   and Council. (European Commission, AppiSToS)
